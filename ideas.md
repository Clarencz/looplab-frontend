# MathematLab — Brand & Experience Direction

## Three Possible Directions

### 1. Operational Modernism
**Very Brief Intro:** A precise, human-centered systems studio inspired by Swiss editorial design and industrial wayfinding. It makes complex digital work feel legible, calm, and decisively managed.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Field Notes Studio
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, research-led consultancy aesthetic using tactile papers, annotations, and documentary photography. It frames every engagement as an investigated opportunity rather than a packaged service.

**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. Signal Architecture
**Very Brief Intro:** A quiet high-tech identity built around technical diagrams, disciplined typography, and structured contrast. It presents MathematLab as a technical partner that turns operational noise into clear systems.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen Direction: Operational Modernism

### Design Movement
**Operational Modernism** combines Swiss International Typographic Style with industrial wayfinding, contemporary editorial art direction, and the visual language of carefully maintained technical documents. It rejects generic agency gloss in favor of calm precision, visible process, and strong hierarchy.

### Core Principles
1. **Make complexity legible.** Every section should reduce ambiguity through direct language, strong grouping, and a clear sequence of thought.
2. **Show the work, not decoration.** Diagrammatic details, numbered steps, and signal lines should communicate the company’s systems mindset without becoming visual clutter.
3. **Be human before technical.** The website must signal a capable partner, not an impersonal software factory. Warm paper tones and candid service language balance the precision.
4. **Use contrast with intent.** Strong type, deep ink backgrounds, and the signature mineral green should identify decisions and calls to action rather than fill the screen with effects.

### Color Philosophy
The palette should feel like a well-used studio desk: **ink black** creates authority and focus; **paper white** provides breathing space and makes information easier to scan; **warm limestone** introduces tactility; and **relay green** marks moments of forward motion, connection, and action. The emotional promise is controlled momentum—not novelty for its own sake.

### Layout Paradigm
The home page follows a **vertical project ledger** rather than a centered marketing stack. A persistent, narrow left information rail carries the brand marker, section numbers, and directional microcopy. Main content is arranged in off-axis editorial blocks: substantial text bands shift against process cards, visual assets occupy unequal columns, and service definitions appear as labeled entries in a working system. On small screens, the ledger collapses into a concise top bar while content retains its sequencing.

### Signature Elements
1. **The relay line:** A thin green route line that travels through key sections and terminates at decision points, suggesting the path from friction to a working system.
2. **Index numerals:** Large, restrained section numbers and compact labels that make the site feel like an operating manual rather than a brochure.
3. **System cards:** Angular, lightly textured panels with small metadata headers such as “01 / DISCOVER” and “BUILD / RELEASE,” reinforcing structured partnership.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should affirm progress. Links underline with a controlled horizontal reveal; project and service cards shift a few pixels to acknowledge selection; the relay line advances only when users reach a new stage. Motion should never obscure content or simulate complexity. Every interactive element must have a clear label and accessible focus state.

### Animation
Use a short custom ease-out curve for entrance and hover transitions. Page sections may reveal through a 20–28px upward drift and opacity transition, staggered by 50ms within a group. Relay lines should draw across no more than 320ms at section entry. Card hovers can use a 2–4px lift and an accent border transition. All nonessential motion must be disabled for users who prefer reduced motion.

### Typography System
**Space Grotesk** is the high-function display face for headlines, navigation, labels, and numerical markers; its technical geometry complements the systems focus. **Source Serif 4** is reserved for long-form supporting statements and selected case-study narrative, making the company read as thoughtful rather than merely technical. Headlines should be compact and left aligned, with deliberate line breaks. Utility labels use uppercase Space Grotesk with generous tracking. Body copy should remain concise, comfortable, and unembellished.

### Brand Essence
**MathematLab is the digital systems partner for organizations ready to turn an important idea or inefficient process into a reliable website, product, or automated workflow.**

**Personality:** Exacting, collaborative, candid.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and outcome-oriented; CTAs sound like the start of a practical working session; microcopy is specific enough to set expectations. The voice avoids hype, jargon, and vague claims of innovation.

> “Bring us the stuck point. We’ll build the system around it.”

> “From first workflow map to a working release.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark should pair the MathematLab name with a compact **relay glyph**: a three-part geometric mark resembling a routed path entering a square node and exiting as a line. It signals analysis, decision, and forward motion without using literal math or code imagery. The mark must stand alone at small sizes and be used as the favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Relay Green — #B7EB2D.** This bright mineral green is used sparingly to mark movement, key actions, and the route through a system.

## Company Positioning & Core Offer

MathematLab is no longer presented as an educational product. It is a **consulting and delivery company** that partners with clients on websites, digital products, bespoke internal systems, and workflow automation.

| Practice | Client outcome | Typical work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Digital Foundations** | A website that clearly explains the business and moves the right people to act. | Strategy, content structure, UX design, responsive websites, analytics foundations. |
| **Product & Systems** | A custom tool or client-facing product designed around how the organization actually works. | Discovery workshops, product design, portals, dashboards, internal operations tools. |
| **Workflow Automation** | Less manual handoff, fewer duplicated tasks, and more reliable operations. | Process mapping, integrations, automation design, notifications, reporting workflows. |
| **Embedded Partnership** | Consistent technical and strategic support after launch. | Roadmaps, iterations, maintenance, performance improvement, feature planning. |

## Home Page Narrative

The page should open with an unambiguous statement that MathematLab partners with organizations to **build the digital systems behind better work**. It should then make the delivery model tangible: identify the friction, design the right approach, build and connect the system, and improve it over time. The site must prioritize a consultation CTA and avoid all educational course, lesson, learning, tutoring, or curriculum language.

## Style Decisions

- **Relay Green #B7EB2D** is a route, decision marker, selected action, or key CTA. It should not be a default full-section background.
- Every supporting system mark should derive from the relay glyph: routed lines, square nodes, directional exits, and numbered operating labels replace generic decorative interface marks.
- Images must balance technical infrastructure with human operational context. Abstract system imagery should be counterweighted with tactile process material or documentary collaboration.
